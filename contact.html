NEED QUICK ANSWERS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a reservation for breakfast?

We keep over half of our café seating reserved strictly for spontaneous walk-in guests throughout the week! On peak weekend mornings between 10am and 1pm, reservations are recommended for groups of 4 or more.

Are dogs allowed on your outdoor patio?

Yes, well-behaved dogs on leashes are warmly welcomed on our outdoor garden patio. We have water bowls and shaded umbrellas ready.

How do you handle severe allergies and gluten intolerance?

Our traditional savory galettes are spun on 100% buckwheat batter with no wheat flour. While we prepare both in our kitchen, our cooks wipe and dedicate distinct griddle spaces for gluten-sensitive orders.

Do you offer gift certificates?

Yes! Digital and physical printed gift cards can be purchased in store or by reaching out to us via email.
