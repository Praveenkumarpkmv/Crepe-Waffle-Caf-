HOUSE FAVORITE COMBOS

Simmered berry compotes & rich chocolates.

Our sweet crêpes are spun extra thin on cast-iron disks heated to 200°C. We top them with house-simmered Ontario fruit compotes, French butter, and rich spreads.

Looking for a gluten-friendly sweet option? We can spin any sweet combination on our 100% buckwheat batter upon request.

ESPRESSO & BEVERAGES

The Perfect Companion to Every Dish

We source single-origin seasonal beans and balance them with rich Ontario dairy or barista-grade oat milk. Whether you crave a silky flat white, an iced shaken espresso, or a loose-leaf Earl Grey, we brew each cup to order.

All syrups (caramel, Madagascar vanilla, lavender) are simmered in-house from whole organic ingredients without chemical additives.

DIETARY INCLUSIVITY

Everyone Deserves Great Brunch

We work meticulously to keep our kitchen welcoming for all dietary preferences.

🌾

Buckwheat Galettes

100% pure buckwheat batter is naturally gluten-free. Ideal for savory lovers looking to avoid gluten.

🥛

Dairy-Free Milks

We offer barista-edition organic oat milk and almond milk at no extra charge on all specialty coffees.

🥑

Plant-Based Options

Ask for our vegan cheese, fresh avocado, and dairy-free coconut whipped cream substitutions.
