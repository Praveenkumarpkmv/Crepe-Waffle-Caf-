SEASONAL INGREDIENTS

From Local Ontario Producers

Fresh produce picked at seasonal peak brings unmatched flavor to our griddles.

We work closely with regional farmers across southern Ontario. From whole milk and salted butter to wild mushrooms and forest blueberries, every plate tells a story of local craftsmanship.

Our buckwheat flour is stone-milled to retain nutty earthiness, making our savory galettes naturally gluten-friendly and deeply satisfying.
