Waffle Specials

Belgian cast-iron waffle irons, pearl sugar crunch on the outside, and airy cloud fluffiness inside.

HOUSE CRAFTED WAFFLES

Crispy outside. Cloud inside.

Cast-iron baked to caramelized golden perfection using century-old Belgian techniques.

🧇

Brioche Yeast Dough

We do not use thin batter. True Belgian Liège waffles are pressed from a rich, butter-infused brioche yeast dough that rises slowly, developing complex fermentation flavors and a pillowy, pull-apart crumb.

🍯

Caramelized Pearl Sugar

Coarse Belgian beet sugar pearls are hand-kneaded into the dough. Under the searing heat of 210°C cast-iron irons, the exterior pearls melt into a crunchy, glossy glaze, while the inner pearls stay softly chewable.

🍗

Savory Infusions & Spices

Beyond sweet creations, we bake sharp aged Canadian cheddar and smoky bacon directly into the grid pockets, then crown them with spiced buttermilk fried chicken, whipped maple butter, and red chili hot honey.

The Cast-Iron Press Technique

Every waffle is made to order on heavy, commercial Belgian waffle irons seasoned over years. No reheated or pre-made waffles ever leave our kitchen — each one arrives at your table steaming hot, aromatic, and crackling with sugar crunch.

AUTHENTIC LIÈGE METHOD

The Belgian pearl sugar secret.

Unlike ordinary runny waffle batter, authentic Liège waffles are crafted from a buttery yeast-leavened brioche dough infused with coarse Belgian pearl sugar crystals.

When pressed into our heavy cast-iron irons, the heat melts the outer sugar into a glass-like caramelized shell, while the pearls trapped inside remain chewy and sweet.
