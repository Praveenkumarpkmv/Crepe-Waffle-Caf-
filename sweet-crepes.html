Sweet Crepes

Paper-thin, featherlight, and spun golden on round griddles. Folded with our favorite sweet indulgences.

CRAFT & PHILOSOPHY

Paper-thin, featherlight, and spun with patience.

Unlike thick pancakes, authentic French sweet crêpes are an exercise in subtlety. Every step in our kitchen honors the Parisian technique.

🌾

12-Hour Rested Batter

Our batter is whipped from stone-ground unbleached flour, farm-fresh eggs, and cold milk, then rested overnight so the proteins relax into silkiness.

🍯

House-Cooked Fillings

We hand-simmer small batches of wild Ontario blueberries, copper-pot salted caramel, and Meyer lemon curd without artificial gelatin or preservatives.

🍫

Artisanal Chocolates

Imported Belgian chocolate ganache and hazelnut cocoa spreads melted fresh on the hot griddle for deep, comforting richness in every fold.

THE ART OF THE ROZELLE

The Parisian technique, perfected in Toronto.

Creating true French crêpes requires precision. Using a traditional wooden spreader (the rozell), our chefs sweep a smooth dollop of thin batter in a swift circular motion across 40cm circular cast-iron plates.

Within seconds, the edges turn golden and lacy while the center stays tender and elastic, ready to absorb rich chocolate, caramel, and berry juices.

RECOMMENDED PAIRING

Warm espresso & sweet confection.
