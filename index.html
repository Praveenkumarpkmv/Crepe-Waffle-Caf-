AUTHENTIC TECHNIQUE

Hand-spun crêpes & crisp Belgian waffles.

From authentic sweet Parisian crêpes smothered in hazelnut cocoa spread and fresh berries to Brittany buckwheat savory galettes with melted Gruyère, our cooks respect the tradition.

Our Belgian waffles are folded with imported pearl sugar, creating pockets of melted caramel crunch with an ultra-airy interior.
