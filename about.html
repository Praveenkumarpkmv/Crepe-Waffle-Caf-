MORE THAN JUST BRUNCH

Our Story & Philosophy

We started with a simple belief: the best moments in life happen unhurried over warm plates and comforting drinks.

OUR BEGINNINGS

Baked with intention in the heart of Toronto.

Crepe & Waffle Café was born from cherished weekend morning rituals — spinning delicate French crêpe batter across cast-iron skillets and pulling rich espresso shots for family, friends, and neighbours.

We envisioned building an inviting haven where brunch doesn't cut off at 11am. Whether you drop by at 8:30am for an energizing breakfast or at 3:00pm for a savory galette and quiet workspace, our griddles stay hot and our doors are wide open.

LOCAL PARTNERSHIPS

From Local Ontario Producers

Every crêpe batter is rested for 12 hours so the proteins relax, creating that featherlight, melt-in-your-mouth texture that characterizes authentic French crêperies.

Our pure maple syrup is harvested from Ontario sugar maples, unblended and unrefined. Our berry compotes are prepared in-house every morning without artificial preservatives or thickeners.

SPECIALTY BARISTA PROGRAM

Espresso brewed with precision and warmth.

A great meal deserves great coffee. We collaborate with independent local roasters to create custom espresso blends that balance dark chocolate undertones and sweet caramel notes.

From micro-textured flat whites to soothing matcha lattes and cold brew, our drink program is crafted to delight coffee lovers and tea connoisseurs alike.
